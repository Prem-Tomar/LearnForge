# Personal AI/ML build — week/day outcomes

Each learner chooses a track from SPECIALIZATIONS.md. Apply these concrete build outcomes to that track, using a small dataset/model/retriever appropriate to its prerequisites. The daily shared architecture topic remains the same for all six.

## Week 21

- Day 141: Choose your AI/ML problem, define its input and useful output, prepare a tiny synthetic dataset, and implement a simple baseline.
- Day 142: Implement typed feature values and a small cleaning/transform step. Explain the units, shapes, missing-value policy, and relevant mathematics.
- Day 143: Version the dataset, feature contract, split policy, and baseline configuration. Keep development and held-out examples separate.
- Day 144: Implement valid training/inference states and one domain invariant for your selected model or retrieval workflow.
- Day 145: Connect your baseline to the shared LearnForge model-capability contract and show one useful result through the product.
- Day six: integrate the selected capability and show a real result plus a failure.
- Day seven: individual fresh variant, bounded implementation repair, and teach-back.

## Week 22

- Day 148: Implement reproducible data ingestion and feature preparation for your chosen track; record source/version and retry-safe processing.
- Day 149: Fit or improve the first real model/retriever on the development data, record its version, and prevent stale configuration overwrites.
- Day 150: Implement a small held-out result report that compares the model with its baseline and shows specific failure cases.
- Day 151: Implement a bounded batch/online inference path and consistent preprocessing; explain behavior under stale replicas or source versions.
- Day 152: Implement resumable feature/index/model-artifact reconciliation and show a controlled rebuild without disrupting the baseline.
- Day six: integrate the selected capability and show a real result plus a failure.
- Day seven: individual fresh variant, bounded implementation repair, and teach-back.

## Week 23

- Day 155: Measure your model or retrieval pipeline: dataset size, runtime, memory, latency, and throughput. State assumptions and the bottleneck.
- Day 156: Implement bounded admission or batch limits for your chosen capability while respecting shared workspace fairness.
- Day 157: Implement a useful baseline/fallback when the model, index, or provider is unavailable or over budget; label uncertainty honestly.
- Day 158: Implement a small model/data/run status view that helps distinguish data drift, dependency failure, and bad prediction evidence.
- Day 159: Implement model/configuration rollback and show that a saved version plus its preprocessing can reproduce a known prediction or outcome.
- Day six: integrate the selected capability and show a real result plus a failure.
- Day seven: individual fresh variant, bounded implementation repair, and teach-back.

## Week 24

- Day 163: Improve the explanation of your selected prediction/recommendation/answer, including uncertainty, evidence, limitations, and next action.
- Day 164: Provide a small repeatable synthetic-data demo that runs the real selected baseline/model path and labels any offline fake separately.
- Day 165: Make one measured model/feature/retrieval improvement and record its quality, latency, cost, and failure trade-off against the baseline.
- Day 166: Explain and hand off your complete vertical capability. A reviewer makes a bounded change after an explicit ownership handoff.
- Day six: integrate the selected capability and show a real result plus a failure.
- Day seven: individual fresh variant, bounded implementation repair, and teach-back.

- Day 171: Repair a real API, concurrency, permission, or resource-lifetime edge in serving your selected capability.
